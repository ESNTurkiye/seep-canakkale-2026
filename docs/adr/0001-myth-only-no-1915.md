# The site stays in the myth layer and does not depict 1915

Çanakkale carries two identities — the Homeric/Trojan one and the 1915 Gallipoli campaign — and the site's visual language is anachronistic humour (classical painting invaded by contemporary objects). That language works on fictional characters and collapses on a living war memory shared by Turkish, Australian and New Zealand audiences, all of whom are represented among ESN delegates. We therefore keep the entire site inside the myth layer: Troy, Homer, the Odyssey, and present-day Çanakkale. 1915 is not depicted, not joked about, and not used as artwork.

## Consequences

- Gallipoli remains part of the real event — it belongs in the Phase 2 programme listing as a plain factual line, with no artwork and no comedic treatment.
- A future reader will notice the omission and assume it was an oversight. It was not.
- If a later host wants to cover 1915, it needs its own visual register (archive photography, stillness, no AI imagery, no anachronism) and its own decision — not an extension of this one.
