# SEEP Çanakkale 2026

The promotional and informational website for SEEP Çanakkale 2026, an ESN regional platform hosted by ESN Çanakkale. This glossary fixes the vocabulary used across the site's copy, its content structure, and the code that renders it.

## The event

**SEEP**:
The South-Eastern European Platform — one of ESN's five regional platforms, covering 15 member countries and the largest of the five by membership. Meets once a year.
_Avoid_: conference, summit, congress

**Platform**:
An ESN regional meeting whose purpose is knowledge sharing, cooperation, capacity building, and briefing sections on ESN International.
_Avoid_: event (too vague when precision matters), gathering

**Host section**:
ESN Çanakkale — the local section running SEEP Çanakkale 2026.
_Avoid_: organiser, host committee

**Section**:
A local ESN branch. Sections send delegates to a platform.
_Avoid_: chapter, branch, university group

**Delegate**:
A person attending SEEP on behalf of their section.
_Avoid_: participant, attendee, guest

**Plenary**:
A session all delegates attend together. Each programme day has one.
_Avoid_: main session, general session, keynote

**Parallel session**:
One of several concurrent sessions delegates choose between. Each programme day has these alongside the plenary.
_Avoid_: workshop, breakout, track

**Programme day**:
Friday, Saturday or Sunday — a day carrying the training programme (plenary + parallel sessions + two coffee breaks). Thursday (arrival, registration, opening ceremony) and Sunday afternoon (departure) are not programme days.
_Avoid_: conference day, session day

**Social programme**:
The evening activities. Distinct from the training programme, which is daytime only.
_Avoid_: nightlife, parties, side events

**Intercultural Night**:
The evening where delegations present food and drink from their own countries.
_Avoid_: culture night, national night

**Turkish Night**:
The evening where the host section presents Turkish food, drink and culture.
_Avoid_: local night, host night

**Organising Committee**:
The sixteen people running SEEP Çanakkale 2026. Always the full name or **OC** — never spelled out differently.
_Avoid_: orga team, organising team, committee

**Daytime venue**:
A place the training programme runs in — where plenaries and parallel sessions happen.
_Avoid_: location, hall, room

## The site

**Phase 1**:
The promotional site: cinematic, myth-led, one primary call to action. Its job is to make a delegate want to come. It shows the hotel, the food and the venues as seduction, not as logistics, and must stand complete on its own.
_Avoid_: teaser, landing page, hype site

**Phase 2**:
The delegate guide: the practical layer. Schedules, addresses, transport times, what to bring, maps. Answers "how do I get there", where Phase 1 answers "do I want to be there". Bolted on later without making Phase 1 look unfinished.
_Avoid_: info site, part two

**Portrait**:
One Organising Committee member's framed image on the OC scene, carrying their name and role. Distinct from the scene artworks, which are landscapes and tableaux.
_Avoid_: headshot, avatar, profile picture

**Scene**:
One full-viewport step of the scroll narrative, with its own artwork and single message. The site is a sequence of scenes.
_Avoid_: section (reserved for the ESN meaning above), slide, panel, page

**Museum**:
The organising metaphor for the whole site. Scrolling is walking through a gallery; each scene is an artwork on its wall that the viewer moves into and back out of.
_Avoid_: gallery (as a name for the site), exhibition

**Artwork**:
The single generated painting belonging to a scene. Scene artworks are landscapes and tableaux; portraits are their own thing.
_Avoid_: image, asset, visual, hero image

**Frame**:
The gilded surround an artwork or portrait hangs in. A separate transparent asset applied over the artwork, never part of it, and never mentioned in an artwork prompt.
_Avoid_: border, gilt, moulding

**Museum label**:
The caption block attached to an artwork or portrait — the small typeset plaque carrying the title and, on portraits, the antique title alongside the real role.
_Avoid_: caption, tooltip, plaque

**Myth layer**:
The Troy / Homeric / Odyssey material that carries the site's visual and comedic language — anachronistic classical painting, contemporary objects intruding.
_Avoid_: theme, concept

## Colour vocabulary

**SEEP green**:
`#7AC143` — the SEEP region's colour and this site's accent, used on dark ground.
_Avoid_: ESN green, accent green, lime
